**IO Processor** : STM32f103

IO required :
- SBUS-RC           : 1xUSART(1), 1xTMR , 1xADC
- PPM-RC            : 1xTMR(1)
- SBUS-Out          : 1xUSART(3), 
- LED               : 1xGPIO
- IO-PWM            : 8xPWM , 1xADC
- SPEKTRUM-pwr-ctrl : 1xGPIO
- IO-Debug          : 1xUSART, 1xSWDx, GPIO (10pin?)
